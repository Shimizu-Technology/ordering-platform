# Questions for Client Meetings

Consolidated list of questions to confirm with Three Squares / B&G Pacific / Latte Stone Cookies when we meet with them.

---

## Three Squares Restaurant

### Menu

- [ ] Is the Donki location menu the same as main location, or limited?
- [ ] Any seasonal/rotating items we should know about?
- [ ] "Soup of the Day" — is there a standard rotation, or truly daily?
- [ ] Are prices current? (We have Breakfast $8.95-$14.95, Mains $11.95-$18.95)
- [ ] Any items frequently 86'd (out of stock)?

### Operations

- [ ] What POS system are you using? (We heard Revel or Clover)
- [ ] How do you currently handle phone orders?
- [ ] Peak hours — when do you get slammed?
- [ ] Do you want order notifications via email, SMS, or both?
- [ ] Staff who will manage orders — how many, what devices?

### Locations

- [ ] Donki location phone number?
- [ ] Any differences in hours between locations?
- [ ] Can customers order from either location for pickup?
- [ ] Any plans for additional locations?

---

## Catering (B&G Pacific)

### Current Process

- [ ] How many catering inquiries do you get per week/month?
- [ ] What's your typical lead time requirement? (2-3 days for specialty items?)
- [ ] Do you provide quotes via email, phone, or in-person?
- [ ] What information do you need to provide a quote?

### Pricing

- [ ] Are the platter prices we found accurate? (Small $35-75, Large $65-140)
- [ ] Bulk bento pricing — minimum quantities?
- [ ] Do you charge for delivery, and if so, how much?
- [ ] Deposit requirements?

### System Needs

- [ ] Simple inquiry form enough, or do you want customers to build custom orders online?
- [ ] Do you want to manage catering inquiries in the same dashboard as restaurant orders?
- [ ] Any integration with existing tools (spreadsheets, CRM, etc.)?

## Latte Stone Cookies

### Products

- [ ] Full product list with current pricing? (We confirmed $11 for 6pc Chocolate Dipped)

- [ ] Seasonal/limited edition items — when do they appear?
- [ ] "Slingstone Cookies" — same brand or separate? Include in our system?
- [ ] "Keepsakes & Gifts" (puzzles, souvenirs) — include or out of scope?

- [ ] Wedding favors — do you need a custom order form?

- [ ] Is there a brand guide (colors, fonts, logo files)?
